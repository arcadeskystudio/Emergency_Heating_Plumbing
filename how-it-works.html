How it works

From burst pipe to booked engineer

Two sides, one callout. Here's what happens for the person with the problem, and for the engineer who fixes it.

01

Describe the problem

Choose Heating or Plumbing, then the specific issue — kitchen leak, no hot water, blocked drain, whatever it is. Add a photo or short video so the engineer arrives with the right parts.

02

Give us your postcode

Search your postcode, pick your address from the list, and tell us how urgent it is — right now, today, or this week.

03

Get matched, not spammed

Your callout is sent to one available, vetted local engineer who covers that job. You won't get five phone calls from five different companies.

04

Track it on your dashboard

See the engineer's details, ETA, and job status — accepted, en route, complete — all in one place.

01

Set your coverage

Tell us which postcodes you cover and whether you do heating, plumbing, or both — right down to specific jobs like boiler repair or bathroom installs.

02

Get exclusive callouts

When a job matches your coverage and you're available, it comes to you first — not shared with four other engineers at once.

03

Accept or pass

See the job type, urgency, and rough location before committing. Accept to unlock the full address and contact details.

04

Build your reputation

Completed callouts build a track record on your profile, so you see more of the jobs that suit you.

Questions

Good to know

Is a callout free to submit?

Yes — submitting a callout costs nothing. Any job pricing is agreed directly with the engineer once they've assessed the work.

Will more than one tradesperson contact me?

No. Each callout is sent to one engineer at a time, so you're not fielding five sales calls after a stressful morning.

Where does Callout operate?

This prototype covers Fife and Edinburgh with five founding engineers, with plans to expand across Scotland.
